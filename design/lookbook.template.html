Motion lookbook

55 customisations across 11 places the app can move, five options each. Tap Play to replay, then Pick as many as you like (several per category is fine). In the app every one is free: equip any mix under More › Motion. Several in one place play at random, while button, celebration and idle effects stack. Cosmetics you earn with ✦ (glass themes, wheel skins, heatmap palettes) never include animation.
