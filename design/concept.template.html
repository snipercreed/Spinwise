Liquid Glass, but actually glass

Frosted blur alone looks like fogged plastic. Real glass bends light at its edges, splits colours slightly, catches a highlight from a light source, and has visible thickness. This concept recreates all four, live. Drag the lens around, move the pointer to move the light, and use the sliders.

What makes it read as glass

1 · Edge refraction

A displacement map bends the backdrop only near the rim, the way a thick curved edge does. The centre stays calm, the edge visibly warps the stripes and chips behind it.

2 · Colour split

Red, green and blue are bent by slightly different amounts, giving the faint rainbow fringe real glass has.

3 · Specular rim

A hairline highlight that is brightest on the edge facing the light and fades around the far side. It follows your pointer, and on a phone it would follow the gyroscope.

4 · Thickness

Inner top highlight, inner bottom bounce, a soft inner glow and two shadows (a wide ambient one and a tight contact one) so each piece reads as a solid object floating above the page.
