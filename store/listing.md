## Full description
Spinwise turns your syllabus into a daily habit. Load your course, spin the wheel, and get one focused topic to revise today.

**Why students like it**
- Spin for today's topic, weighted towards what you know least or are due to review
- A GitHub-style streak grid shows every day you studied
- Track how revised each topic is, from 0 to 100%, with colour-coded subjects
- Suggestions that follow on from what you just revised
- Load your whole spec from Claude (or paste any list, CSV or outline) and start in a minute
- Earn ✦ for revising and unlock themes, wheel skins and heatmap palettes
- Home-screen widget with your streak and progress
- Sync between your phone and tablet using a file in your own Google Drive or other cloud storage
- Share a checklist with a friend in one tap
- Light and dark mode, 55 optional animations, reduced-motion friendly

**Private by design.** Everything stays on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking.
