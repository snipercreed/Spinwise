Spinwise Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date]

Spinwise (“the app”) is a revision tracker. This policy explains what happens to your information.

What the app stores

Your subjects, topics, notes, revision history, rewards and settings. This data is stored on your device only.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in or registration
  • No advertising
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services
  • No data is sent to the developer or to any server operated by the developer

Optional features that move data, only when you choose them

  • Sync across devices: you pick a file in a cloud storage app you already use (for example Google Drive). The app writes your data to that file. That provider’s own privacy policy applies to the file. You can stop syncing at any time in More › Sync across devices.
  • Share: when you tap Share, the app hands text (a checklist’s topics, or a streak summary) to the app you choose in Android’s share sheet.
  • Backup / copy: copying a backup or a prompt places text on your clipboard.
  • Android Auto Backup: your device may include the app’s data in your Google backup, according to your device settings.

Update checks (direct-download version only)

The copy of the app installed directly from GitHub (not from Google Play) can check for new versions. When it does, it makes a request to GitHub to read the latest release information and, if you tap Update, downloads the new version from GitHub. The request carries no account, identifiers or data from the app. You can switch the automatic check off in More › App updates. The Google Play version contains no update check and has no internet permission.

Children

The app is suitable for students. It does not collect personal information from anyone.

Your control

Delete the app to remove all local data. Delete the sync file in your cloud storage to remove synced data.
